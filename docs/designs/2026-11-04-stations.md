# Stations

Status: built for 1.8.0, 2026-11-04. Code: `HammerStation`, `HammerStations`, `HammerPublic.carry`, ops 24 and 25.

An artist goes on air with their public songs, in order or shuffled. Everyone tuned in hears the same song at the same
moment, like radio. No server runs it.

## The record is the station

A station is one record signed by the artist: title, a note of up to 140 letters, start, end, revision, and the
songs with their lengths in milliseconds. From those and the clock, any phone works out what is on now and how far
in. Nothing has to stay online to keep it going, and a copy held by anyone is as good as the artist's.

- One station per artist. A newer revision replaces the older one, whether it carries a new note or ends the station.
- It loops until it ends, and ends by itself after 30 days.
- A host offers a station only when it shares every song on it under the same artist's signature, so a listener
  never meets a gap.

## Carrying

Any host that shares every song on a station may carry the artist's signed copy, so it stays on air while the
artist's phone sleeps. Copies move three ways:

1. **Handed on (op 25, `CARRY_STATION`).** Anyone may hand a host a station. The host keeps it only when the
   signature checks, it shares every song, and the copy is newer than its own. A copy that has already ended is
   taken only to replace a live one. So a host can't be filled with stations it has no part in, and nobody but
   the artist can put a station back on air.
2. **From the artist's phone.** Going on air, a new note, or going off air hands the newest copy to every host
   Discover has seen sharing all its songs.
3. **From listeners and helpers.** After each Discover refresh a phone keeps a copy when it shares the songs itself,
   and hands the newest copy to hosts holding an older one or none (at most 12 handings a refresh). Every ten
   minutes a helper asks up to 16 hosts it knows for their stations, takes newer copies, and hands its own newer
   copies back.

An ended station is still handed out for a week, marked by its end, so copies elsewhere learn it ended.

## Honest limits

- A station keeps going without the artist only once a host that shares all its songs has a copy. If nobody
  rehosts the songs, the station stops when the artist's phone stops hosting.
- A carrier that hears from nobody keeps playing an older copy until it hears of a newer one or the station's end
  passes. Going off air reaches carriers as fast as copies move, not at once.
- Hidden artists' stations aren't carried through the mixnet yet.
- Listener counts and choosing songs or albums for a station are not built yet.

## Tests

`peer/test/HammerIntegration.java`: timing and looping, tampering, replacement by revision, ended stations still
handed out, a host without the songs refusing, a host with them carrying, a newer note and going off air reaching
the carrier, a carrier unable to restart a station without the artist's key, and an ended station not taken by a
host that never held it. A two-host run on ops (artist on air, helper rehosts and is handed the station, artist shuts
down) left the helper with the station on air.
