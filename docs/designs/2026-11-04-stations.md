# Stations

Status: built for 1.8.0, 2026-11-04. Code: `HammerStation`, `HammerStations`, `HammerPublic.carry` and `listening`, ops 24 to 26, mixnet op 5.

An artist goes on air with their public songs, in order or shuffled. Everyone tuned in hears the same song at the same
moment, like radio. No server runs it.

## The record is the station

A station is one record signed by the artist: title, a note of up to 140 letters, start, end, revision, and the
songs with their lengths in milliseconds. From those and the clock, any phone works out what is on now and how far
in. Nothing has to stay online to keep it going, and a copy held by anyone is as good as the artist's.

- One station per artist. A newer revision replaces the older one, whether it carries a new note or ends the station.
- It loops until it ends, and ends by itself after 30 days.
- A host offers a station only when it shares every song on it under the same artist's signature, so a listener
  never meets a gap.

## Carrying

Any host that shares every song on a station may carry the artist's signed copy, so it stays on air while the
artist's phone sleeps. Copies move three ways:

1. **Handed on (op 25, `CARRY_STATION`).** Anyone may hand a host a station. The host keeps it only when the
   signature checks, it shares every song, and the copy is newer than its own. A copy that has already ended is
   taken only to replace a live one. So a host can't be filled with stations it has no part in, and nobody but
   the artist can put a station back on air.
2. **From the artist's phone.** Going on air, a new note, or going off air hands the newest copy to every host
   Discover has seen sharing all its songs.
3. **From listeners and helpers.** After each Discover refresh a phone keeps a copy when it shares the songs itself,
   and hands the newest copy to hosts holding an older one or none (at most 12 handings a refresh). Every ten
   minutes a helper asks up to 16 hosts it knows for their stations, takes newer copies, and hands its own newer
   copies back.

An ended station is still handed out for a week, marked by its end, so copies elsewhere learn it ended.

## Choosing what plays

Going on air plays every song you share, or one of your albums, in order or shuffled.

## When nobody shares your songs yet

The artist page says how many other hosts were seen carrying your station. When none were, it says the station may
stop when your phone stops hosting, and offers **Ask helpers**: each song goes through the mixnet to up to three
helpers, the same way a hidden artist's does, the first song to whichever helpers take it and every later one to the
same helpers, so they hold them all; then they are handed the station (mixnet op 5). Helpers take up to 25 songs and
32 MB a song from one artist. A new note or going off air also goes to those helpers through the mixnet.

## Hidden names

A hidden artist can go on air too, from the Library's sharing screen (**Hidden station**). The station is signed with
the hidden name's key and goes only through the mixnet to the helpers holding that name's songs, so this phone never
hosts it. A new hidden song is offered first to the helpers that hold the name's other songs, so one helper can carry
them all. The station plays the songs the best-stocked helper holds.

## Going off air quickly

A helper that takes a newer revision passes it straight on to the hosts it last saw holding an older copy. A
listener's phone asks up to three hosts between songs and follows the newest copy, so one stale host can't keep a
station on air for it.

## Listeners

A tuned-in phone makes a random tag for that tune-in and tells the host it is listening (op 26). Each host answers
the tags it heard in the last ten minutes, as short hashes. The artist's page joins the answers of this phone and the
hosts it knows, so a listener who moved between hosts counts once. A tag says nothing about who is listening. Hosts
reached only through the mixnet aren't asked, so the count can be low, never high.

## Honest limits

- A station keeps going without the artist only once a host that shares all its songs has a copy.
- Going off air reaches carriers as fast as copies move, not at once. A carrier that hears from nobody plays an
  older copy until the station's end passes.
- Hidden stations, Ask helpers and the mixnet route have been tested only on their own, not yet through a
  real mixnet.
- A song longer than ten minutes can drop its listener out of the count until the next song.
