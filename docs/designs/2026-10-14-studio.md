# Studio: a whole music studio inside Killa YuE

14 October 2026. Agreed with Alexander in chat; this note records it. Each phase gets its own short spec before it is built.

## What it is for

An artist makes a song in Killa YuE, then takes it into the Studio. There they split it into stems, record their own voice and instruments over it, loop, edit, play MIDI instruments, mix, master and export. All of it runs on the phone, offline, for free. The Studio replaces Killa Studio, our earlier studio app. We keep that app's proven parts and its look, and rebuild the rest properly.

North star for the first release ("remix and play along"): open any song, split it into stems, then:
- mute, solo and level the stems;
- record vocals and instruments over them (mic, USB audio interface, or another app's sound);
- edit (move, trim, split, duplicate, undo), loop, and record takes;
- play MIDI instruments;
- add effects, mix, master and export.

## Why it is one of a kind

- **Your own AI songs, taken apart on the phone.** Stems from any song with no upload and no account. Then play inside your own song.
- **Sample anything.** Record another app's sound straight onto a track (Android 10 and up, with Android's consent prompt), alongside the mic and USB interfaces.
- **Sheet music that knows the song.** Stems feed the sheet music: the clean vocal is what lets words go under every note, proven by a checker.
- **Proven, not claimed.** Every phase ships with checks run on the phone:
  - measured round-trip latency;
  - a test tone recorded and found exactly where it belongs;
  - every audio glitch counted, with zero allowed;
  - stems compared with the reference model.
- **Dressed like the rest of Killa YuE.** Gold, Neon District and Foundry all apply to the Studio, plus new Pixella art for its own controls in each skin.

## Decisions

- **Inside Killa YuE**, not a second app. A Studio tab opens the Studio screen (its own activity and Java package, `ai.jackknife.killayue.studio`), in portrait or landscape. Every song's menu gets "Open in Studio".
- **Rebuild and keep the gems.** Killa Studio's code was audited on 14 October:
  - its app code is minified one-liners in a god class;
  - its newest builds never ran on a phone;
  - its audio engine renders only 16–20 ms ahead on an ordinary thread that also reads the disk;
  - recordings have no latency compensation;
  - 0 of its 160 features passed acceptance.

  We keep:
  - its EQ, filter, compressor and delay DSP, its bus and send routing, and its automation curves;
  - its crash-safe export and recording recovery;
  - its resumable SHA-256 model installers;
  - the Demucs stem runtime;
  - its analytic audio tests.

  The rest is new.
- **The same screens, clean code.** Arrange, piano and piano roll, sampler pads, mixer, effects and beats keep Killa Studio's layout and feel. Each screen is its own class and redraws only what changed. Assets come from Killa YuE's skins; Killa Studio art is reused only where it looks right; new controls get Pixella art for each skin, made with that skin's assets as the reference.
- **Models on our own Hugging Face** (JackKnifeAI), pinned by size and SHA-256. Each one downloads the first time its feature is used. Killa Studio's two-speaker separator is dropped: its weights are share-alike and it made speech worse.

## Architecture

```
Studio screens (Java)  ──commands──>  Project (Java, pure, tested)  ──edits──>  Engine bridge (JNI)
      ^                                     |  autosave + backup                     |  lock-free queue
      |  status: position, meters,          v                                        v
      +---- underruns, latency  <───  project folder  <── recordings ──  Audio engine (C++, libstudio.so)
                                                                                     ^
AI jobs process (stems now; beats and voices later) ── finished stems ──> project ───+
```

- **Project** (Java, no Android in it, so it can be tested on ops):
  - tracks, clips, loop regions, sends, effect chains and automation, every one with a stable ID;
  - undo and redo as a list of commands, not snapshots;
  - autosave in the background after edits, writing a temporary file and a backup; loading falls back to the backup if the file is damaged.
  - Folder: `KillaYuE/studio/<project>/project.json` plus `audio/` (stems, recordings, imports).
- **Audio engine** (C++, Oboe, Apache-2.0):
  - one full-duplex stream for playback and recording;
  - the mix is rendered inside the real-time callback, with no locks, allocation or file reads there;
  - a disk thread streams clips ahead through lock-free rings;
  - edits arrive as messages on a lock-free queue and apply within one buffer, without restarting the stream;
  - the stream reopens itself when the audio route changes (headphones, Bluetooth, USB);
  - the device's native sample rate, with a proper band-limited resampler for files at other rates (Killa YuE songs are 44.1 kHz) and constant-power pan;
  - master: a soft limiter with lookahead, never a hard clip;
  - an underrun counter, callback-time statistics and measured input/output latency, all reported to the screen and to the tests.
- **Recording:**
  - **Sources:** mic, USB interface, or another app (MediaProjection playback capture; an app that forbids capture records silence, and the Studio says so instead of pretending).
  - **While recording:** monitoring through the engine, count-in and click, punch in and out, and loop recording into takes.
  - **Placement:** every take is placed using the measured round-trip latency. Calibration plays a click and finds it in the input.
  - **Safety:** recordings stream to disk and survive a crash.
- **MIDI:**
  - **Input:** Android's MIDI API for USB and Bluetooth keyboards, read on its own thread with timestamps kept, and scheduled to the exact sample.
  - **On screen:** keys.
  - **Instruments:** a synth (rebuilt to be efficient: band-limited oscillators, not per-sample `sin`) and sampled instruments (sfizz, with seeks prepared off the real-time thread).
  - **Editing:** MIDI recordings become notes on a piano roll.
- **AI jobs process:** stems use htdemucs 4-stem (vocals, drums, bass, other) through ExecuTorch on the GPU, in a separate process like Killa YuE's engine. One heavy AI job runs at a time, and recording and playback always come first. Finished stems arrive as WAV files and become tracks. Beats (ACE-Step) and voices (VoxCPM2) come later as experimental jobs; on today's evidence they are slow.

## Proof, for every phase

- **Host tests on ops:** DSP and the engine's mixing against closed-form expectations (Killa Studio's analytic tests carried over), the project model, undo, and recovery from damaged files.
- **On the phone**, a test entry that is not in the release build:
  - round-trip latency measured;
  - a test tone recorded and found within 1 ms of where it belongs;
  - playback of 16 tracks with effects for ten minutes with zero underruns;
  - route changes survived;
  - stems from the phone compared with the reference PyTorch model on ops.
- **Checkers break things on purpose** to show they catch mistakes, as with the sheet music.
- **Alexander listens.** A phase is done when it passes its checks and sounds right to him.

## Build order

1. **Remix:** the engine and the Studio screen, stems, mixer and export. First measure Demucs's speed and memory on the S25, which was never done.
2. **Play along:** recording from mic, USB and other apps; latency calibration; loop takes; editing.
3. **MIDI and instruments:** keyboards, synths, sampled instruments, the piano roll.
4. **Sampler, pads and beats:** chopping, pads, the step sequencer.
5. **Effects rack, automation and mastering:** the reverb, saturation and limiter rebuilt.
6. **AI voices and beats**, as experimental jobs.

Model hosting on Hugging Face goes alongside, for each model as its phase needs it, after a licence check.

## Risks to watch

- **The GPU is shared** with song making and cover painting. The Studio waits rather than fighting for it.
- **Memory:** Killa Studio claimed 5 GB for stems without evidence; measure before promising.
- **APK size:** ExecuTorch and the instrument packs; keep big things as downloads.
- **Android 9 phones** get everything except recording other apps, which needs Android 10.
- **Licences:**
  - SoundTouch is LGPL: offline use only, as a replaceable library, or avoided.
  - The ACE-Step, Qwen and VoxCPM2 weights are checked before hosting.
